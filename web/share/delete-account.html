For the Splitbit app (app.splitbit.splitbit)

You can delete your account here without opening the app. The easiest way inside the app is Settings, delete my account.

what gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, avatar colour, bKash number and notification token.
  • Your groups, your friends list and your notifications.
  • Every bill you made, with its items, shares and payments. Friends who had its share link will no longer see it.
  • Your login.

On bills other people made, your name stays as plain text so their totals still add up. Encrypted backups of our database host can take up to about 30 days to disappear. This cannot be undone.

1. confirm it is you

Enter the email you log in with. If you use Google, use your Google email. We send a 6 digit code.

2. enter the code

3. delete

Type delete to confirm.

Your account has been deleted. You can close this page.
