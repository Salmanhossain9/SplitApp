privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Splitbit helps friends split a bill in Bangladesh. This page explains what the Splitbit app and its share pages collect, why, who sees it, and how you delete it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

what we collect

whatwhy
Email address, or the email and name of your Google account if you sign in with GoogleTo log you in with a 6 digit code or with Google, and to know which bills are yours.
Your name and avatar colourShown to the people you split bills with.
bKash number (optional)Shown on the bills you share, so friends know where to send money. We never touch the money.
Bills: place name, items, prices, who had what, VAT and service charge, who has paidThe core of the app: working out and showing each person's share.
Names of the people you add, groups, and an optional phone number for a friendTo split bills with them. A phone number is only used to open a WhatsApp message from your own phone; we do not message anyone ourselves.
Notifications and a push token for your phone (if you allow notifications)To tell you when a friend has paid or when a bill is shared with you.

receipt photos

When you scan a receipt, the photo is read on your phone with Google ML Kit text recognition. The photo is not uploaded to us and we do not keep it. Only the items and prices you confirm are saved as part of the bill. The camera is used only when you tap scan.

share pages

When you send a bill, the app makes a link. Anyone who has the link can open that one bill without logging in and sees the place, the items, each person's name and amount, and your bKash number. Do not post a link where you do not want strangers to read it. The link is long and random, so it cannot be guessed.

who we share data with

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools. We use a few services to run the app, and they handle data only for us:

  • Supabase: database, login and the server functions.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • An email provider: to send your login code.
  • Netlify: hosts the share and policy pages. Like any web host, it sees the IP address of whoever opens a page.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your bills can only be read by you, the people on that bill, and anyone you give the share link to.

how long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, groups, friends, notifications and every bill you made are deleted straight away. Our database host may keep encrypted backups for a short time (typically up to 30 days) before they are overwritten.

On a bill that someone else made, your name stays as plain text (no link to an account) so the totals on their bill still add up.

delete your account

In the app: Settings, delete my account. Or on the web: delete your account. Both do the same thing and cannot be undone.

your choices

  • You can change your name, avatar colour and bKash number in Settings, or leave the bKash number empty.
  • You can turn off notifications or the camera in your phone settings.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have something corrected, write to us.

children

Splitbit is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for big changes tell you in the app.
